# Syphon landing page

## Booklet

Upright VC Henrietta, brown ink, warm paper and amber from the glass. The cover pairs the light app icon with the headline. The app and Audio File icons use the upright approved renders. The source objects share a surface and cast small directional shadows. On phones each caption sits beside its object. The feature spreads alternate text and native app details on desktop and follow a single reading order on phones.

## Interaction

The image itself opens a captioned dialog by click, tap or keyboard. Both the cover and the bottom of the page offer “Get Syphon for Mac”. Zoom in centers the relevant controls at a readable scale on phones and desktops. The Close button and Escape return focus to the originating screenshot. Each source icon loops while it is onscreen in a visible tab. Each Audio File capsule has its own slow swirl and a small bubble that rises below the surface. The glass and liquid levels stay still. Reduce Motion displays the still artwork.

## Support and Privacy

Both pages use the booklet’s paper, upright Henrietta headings and system-font body copy. A thin rule separates each topic. On wider screens the heading sits beside the instructions. On phones it sits directly above them. The wordmark returns to the landing page and the footer marks the current page.
