# Syphon on the web

## Product and audience

Syphon turns synth patches into playable sampled instruments for musicians using an Apple silicon or Intel Mac running macOS 14 or later. Sources include AU and VST3 instruments, MIDI hardware with an audio return, and imported audio. It writes WAV and AIFF samples and maps instruments for SFZ and Decent Sampler.

## Evidence

The local Vite page uses the approved artwork and screenshots of variations, named presets and the loop editor. One Dexed AU run captured three notes at two velocities, two CC1 settings, two patches and two rounds. The result was 48 WAVs and four SFZ instruments. A separate held C4 from Dexed illustrates a 2.41-second Seam loop. The page also describes the three audio-file pitch modes and set-wide normalization.

## Action

“Get Syphon for Mac” points to a local availability notice until a direct download URL is configured. The intended base is /syphon/. The user chose the Henrietta booklet design.
