Capture the variations.

Velocity layers
Pick the keys and playing strengths. Syphon maps the notes and velocities between them.
MIDI CC
Record the same keys at several mod-wheel or filter settings. Each setting gets its own instrument.
Round robin
Record 2–16 takes per note and velocity. Repeated notes can cycle through the takes in order or pick one at random.

Keep the patch names.

Software synths
Choose patches by name from your AU or VST3’s preset list, or sample them all in one run. Exported samples and instruments use the synth’s own patch names.
MIDI hardware
Step through channels, banks and program changes. Enter your hardware patch names once for the whole run.
Long captures
Render plug-ins offline or in real time. If a run is interrupted, Syphon can resume with just the missing takes.

Loop the sustain.

Keep the attack and release you recorded. Let Automatic find a loop, or choose how Syphon makes the join.

Seam
Finds a natural repeat in a steady tone.
Crossfade
Blends the end back into the start for moving pads and strings.
Spectral
Rebuilds a repeating sustain from the spectrum of a noisy or chorused sound.

Pitch, trim and export.

Pitch a recording
Modern holds the duration. Varispeed changes speed and pitch together. Retro uses adjustable 10–200 ms grains.
Prepare the set
Trim silence, add fades and normalize the whole set together so the soft notes stay soft.
Export an instrument
Write WAV or AIFF with loop markers, or a mapped SFZ or Decent Sampler instrument with velocity layers, round robins and releases.
