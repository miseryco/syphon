Syphon Support

Privacy policy

Syphon captures and processes sound on your Mac. The app works without an account and includes no ads, tracking or usage analytics.

Updated November 20, 2026

Your recordings and exports

Syphon reads the audio file or instrument you choose, processes the takes locally and writes the result to your chosen folder. Misery & Company Ltd. receives no recordings, patch names or MIDI messages from the app. If you export to a shared drive or a cloud-synced folder, that folder’s sharing and sync settings apply.

MIDI and audio input

In MIDI Device mode, Syphon sends notes and controller messages to the MIDI destination you select. It requests macOS Microphone permission to record the audio input you select, including inputs on USB interfaces. Plug-in capture and audio-file processing work without this permission. You can change it in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone.

Plug-ins and their libraries

Syphon scans installed instruments and loads the AU or VST3 you choose in a helper process on your Mac. An instrument may need its own presets or sample libraries. macOS can ask on Syphon’s behalf for access to Documents, Downloads or an external or network drive. Any account, licensing or online service a third-party plug-in uses is covered by its developer’s privacy policy.

What Syphon saves

Syphon stores app preferences on your Mac. Its plug-in scan cache, saved presets and temporary runs live in ~/Library/Application Support/Syphon/. A preset can remember note ranges, routing, the output folder and supported plug-in settings. It contains no audio recordings or sample libraries. You can delete individual presets in Settings → Presets.

While capturing, Syphon keeps temporary audio, source settings, file paths and a record of completed takes. After a full export, Done clears that run’s working files. You can resume or discard an interrupted or partially exported run in Unfinished Runs.
