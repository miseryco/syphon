Make your first instrument

  1. Open Syphon on a Mac running macOS 14 or later. Click the source name in the title bar. Choose Plugin for an AU or VST3 instrument, MIDI Device for hardware, or Audio File for a recording.
  2. Load your sound. Under Notes, choose the keys to sample. For a plug-in or hardware synth, set the velocities you want. Click Preview to hear a processed note.
  3. In Output, choose a folder and format, then click Generate. When the capture finishes, Show in Finder opens the result.

WAV and AIFF give you individual samples. For SFZ or Decent Sampler, keep the .sfz or .dspreset file beside its Samples folder when you move the instrument.

A plug-in is missing or silent

In the plug-in picker, click Rescan after installing an instrument. Use Add VST3… for a VST3 you keep outside the usual plug-in folders.

If the instrument loads silently, open Show Editor, select a patch and try Audition. If Audition works but Preview is silent, open Settings → General, set Plugin rendering mode to Realtime compatibility and try Preview again.

My hardware synth is silent

Syphon sends notes over MIDI and records the audio coming back. Set MIDI destination and the channel your synth listens on. Route the synth’s audio output to your interface, or select its USB audio input if it provides one.

Choose that Audio input and the matching Input channels. Play a note on the synth and check the input meter. For recording permission, click Allow Audio Input…. If access was denied, use Open Privacy Settings and enable Syphon under Privacy & Security → Microphone.

A recording plays in the wrong key

In Audio File, set Original note to the pitch of the recording. Preview a different key to check it. Choose Modern to keep the duration, Varispeed to change speed with pitch, or Retro to adjust the grain size.

A held note cuts off

Under Output → Processing, set Loop to Automatic and preview a sustained note. If Syphon finds a repeat, the waveform shows where it loops. Drag the handles to adjust it, then generate the instrument. For a slowly evolving synth patch, try a longer Hold to capture more of the sustain.

Recover an interrupted capture

Open Unfinished Runs with the clock button in the title bar. Load the original plug-in and patch, reconnect the hardware, or make the original audio file available. Resume records the missing takes.

Use Export Partial… to save the takes you already have, or Discard to remove the temporary run. If every take was recorded before the interruption, choose Export to finish writing the instrument.

Remove Syphon

Finish or discard any unfinished runs, quit Syphon and move the app from Applications to the Trash.

To remove saved presets and working files too, choose Go → Go to Folder… in Finder and enter ~/Library/Application Support/. Copy any presets you want to keep out of the Syphon folder, then move that folder to the Trash. Exports saved elsewhere stay where you put them.

Contact support

Email Misery & Company at syphon@misery.co. Include your macOS version, the version under Syphon → About Syphon, and the exact message or behaviour you saw.

Tell us which source you chose. For a plug-in, give its name and format (AU or VST3). For hardware, name your synth and audio interface.
